Health & Illnesses 💊

Present Perfect: Ever, Never, For, Since

Vocabulary: Health & Injuries

backache cold cough earache fever flu headache sore throat stomach ache toothache
break (broken) bruise burn (burnt) fall (fallen) hurt medicine injury

🌟 Ever & Never

Usamos ever (alguna vez) para preguntar por experiencias y never (nunca) para decir que no hemos hecho algo.

Question (Ever)

Have you ever had a toothache?

Negative Idea (Never)

I have never broken my finger.

⏱️ For & Since

Usamos for para una cantidad de tiempo y since para el punto de inicio.

For (Duration: 3 days, 2 weeks...)

I have felt ill for three days.

Since (Starting point: Monday, 2015...)

She has had a fever since Tuesday.

Adventures & Sports 🏕️

Advice with: Should

Vocabulary: Adventure & Travel

cycle journey mountain Northern Lights pyramid safari sail tour turtle whale
go horse riding go kayaking go rock climbing go scuba diving go skydiving go snowboarding go surfing go white-water rafting
competition skis speed track

👍 Affirmative

Subject + should + verb

You should go scuba diving!

👎 Negative

Subject + shouldn't + verb

You shouldn't touch the whales.

🤔 Question

Should + Subject + verb?

Should I pack my skis?

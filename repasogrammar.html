The Passive Voice (Voz Pasiva)

Utilizamos la voz pasiva cuando no sabemos quién realiza una acción o cuando el agente ejecutor carece de importancia en el contexto.

Present Simple Passive

  • Estructura: is / isn't o are / aren't + participio pasado.
  • Is honey made by birds?
  • No, honey isn't made by birds. It's made by bees!

Past Simple Passive

  • Estructura: was / wasn't o were / weren't + participio pasado.
  • An egg was added to the water.
  • The salt water and the fresh water weren't stirred.
Look! Si queremos decir quién hizo la acción, añadimos la partícula by después del participio pasado.
✏️ Actividades de Práctica: Passive Voice
1. How ________ jetpacks ________ (present passive / use)?
2. ________ the water ________ (past passive / heat) before adding the egg?
3. Why ________ the salt ________ (past passive / add) to the fresh water?
4. Honey is made ________ bees, not by birds.
5. The liquids ________ (past passive negative / stir) by the scientist.
Resultado: -- / 5

Future in the Past (Was / Were going to)

Usamos estas estructuras para referirnos a planes u organización en el pasado que luego se modificaron o cancelaron.

was / were going to

  • I was going to get you a board game, but I remembered you love making things.

wasn't / weren't going to

  • I wasn't going to learn to play the saxophone, but I loved the sound of it.
Look! Recuerda usar siempre una coma antes de introducir la palabra but. El verbo después de but puede ir en presente o en pasado.
✏️ Actividades de Práctica: Future in the Past
1. I ________ buy a board game, but I changed my mind.
2. He ________ learn the saxophone, but he loved its sound.
3. ¿Qué signo de puntuación es obligatorio poner justo antes de la palabra "but"?
4. They ________ (plan affirmative) travel yesterday, but the plans changed.
5. We ________ (plan negative) attend the concert, but then we decided to go.
Resultado: -- / 5

Second Conditional (Segundo Condicional)

Lo usamos para hablar de eventos en el presente o futuro que son irreales o improbables de que ocurran.

  • Estructura: If + past simple (en la condición) y would / wouldn't + verbo (en el resultado).
Look! Puedes cambiar el orden de la frase condicional. Ponemos una coma después de la cláusula con if si esta va primero. Recuerda además que la contracción wouldn't equivale a would not.
If I met this artist, I would ask him questions.

2. If we ________ (not have) school tomorrow, I would go to the art gallery.
3. If we didn't have school tomorrow, I ________ (not go) to an art gallery.
4. Cláusula condicional: "If I ________ (meet) this artist..." (Completa el tiempo verbal adecuado).
5. ¿Cuál es la forma completa y sin contraer de la palabra "wouldn't"?

Third Conditional (Tercer Condicional)

Se utiliza para hablar de acciones imaginadas u ficticias en el pasado que no ocurrieron en la realidad.

Third conditional statements

  • Usa el past perfect en la cláusula con *if*, y would / wouldn't have + past participle en el resultado.

Third conditional questions

  • Sirven para preguntar por eventos imaginados que no pasaron. Puedes alterar también el orden de la pregunta.
Look! La contracción de letra 'd puede ser una abreviación de had o de would dependiendo del contexto.
*If I'd (had) known there were acrobats, I'd (would) have bought a ticket.*
✏️ Actividades de Práctica: Third Conditional
1. If you had come, you ________ (see) the fireworks.
2. I ________ (not have) my face painted if I'd gone.
3. ________ (you / wear) a costume if you had gone?
4. What would you have done if someone ________ (ask) you to join the parade?
5. En la frase "If I'd known...", ¿qué palabra representa la contracción "'d"?

Reported Speech (Estilo Indirecto)

Cambiamos los tiempos verbales de las oraciones para reproducir las declaraciones hechas por otra persona.

Direct Speech Reported Speech
"I'm going to..." He said he was going to...
"I saw a leopard." She said she had seen a leopard.
"I've read a book." He said he had read a book.
"I will take more photos." She said she would take more photos.

Reported speech: said and told

Usamos said para reportar diálogos generales, mientras que usamos told para reportar mandatos directos. Requerimos un objeto explícito tras el uso de told pero no con said.

Look! El profesor nos dijo que no hiciéramos algo utilizando la estructura told us not to + infinitivo.
✏️ Actividades de Práctica: Reported Speech
1. Direct: "I'm going to the park." -> Indirect: He said he ________ the park.
2. Direct: "I saw a leopard." -> Indirect: She said she ________ a leopard.
3. Direct: "I will take more photos." -> Indirect: She said she ________ take more photos.
4. "The tour guide ________ us to be very quiet." (¿Said o Told?)
5. "The teacher told us ________ run in the hallway." (Completa la orden negativa)

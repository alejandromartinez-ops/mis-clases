The Present Perfect

We use the Present Perfect for actions that happened in the past but are connected to the present!

Game Vocabulary

avatar button computer controller keyboard laptop monitor mouse speaker tablet
partner player winner

Important Past Participles

collect ➔ collected guess ➔ guessed move ➔ moved pick ➔ picked race ➔ raced score ➔ scored drop ➔ dropped hide ➔ hidden keep ➔ kept
✅
👍

Affirmative

Subject + have / has + Past Participle

I, You, We, They

The players have collected the coins.

He, She, It

The winner has kept the laptop.

❌
👎

Negative

Subject + haven't / hasn't + Past Participle

I, You, We, They

We haven't dropped the controller.

He, She, It

He hasn't guessed the word.

❓
🤔

Question

Have / Has + Subject + Past Participle?

Have you scored a point?

Yes, I have. No, I haven't.

Has she picked an avatar?

Yes, she has. No, she hasn't.
